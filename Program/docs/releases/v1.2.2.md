A fix-up update to 1.2, "The Dark Backward", built from what testers ran into this week: meeting up in person, pictures, the feed and a few world settings. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Meeting up in person**
- The plan card on **Today** now says how a meetup works: when it's time, you're there together, and the chat is where it happens. There's no separate step to get there.
- **Go now** starts a meetup planned for later today straight away, when nothing of theirs is in the way. **Stay longer** adds an hour while their next hour is free, up to three times.
- A date no longer ends in the middle of a conversation. If you're still talking near the end, it runs on for another hour by itself when they're free, and near the end they'll say whether they can stay a bit longer or have to head off.
- Afterwards they're on their way home for a while (on foot, by subway, tram, carriage and so on, depending on the city and the distance), and the chat shows when they'll be back. Texts sent then come from the road.
- In person, actions written between asterisks show as *italic action text*, and the message box says **Talk to (name)**.

**You in the pictures**
- Your persona now has **How you look** and **Your picture**. Upload one, or make one from how you look with this world's image setup.
- Pictures of moments you share, like an outing, a visit or a big day, show you beside your companion. **Settings > Images** has **Show me in pictures we're in together** for each world.
- While you're together, a picture shows the moment as you see it, not a selfie, so your companion is no longer drawn twice.
- Pictures show the place they're at, from the world's lore and the city, and a work shift happens at the workplace they named.
- A photo your companion promised in chat follows the scene they described, not the scene and pose of their profile picture.

**The feed**
- Your companion never asks the same question twice, and has far more questions and passing thoughts to post, each fitting the era of their world.
- Coming back after time away, each moment gets its own post at the time it happened, instead of one long catch-up post.
- Reactions to a friend's post come from people who know that friend. A rival may keep quiet or get in a dig: the new **Trolls and snark** slider on the **Realism** tab sets how much (Rivals only by default). Nobody snipes at a birthday, a question, or someone sick or grieving.
- A day always has at least one post from the circle, even when it's quiet.
- Small wording fixes: posts about themselves say "my", event reminders read right, and dinner isn't breakfast.

**Your worlds**
- **Drama** on the **Realism** tab is now set for each world on its own, and storyline moments for different companions happen at their own times of day instead of all at once.
- The **Worlds** page shows which worlds are **Paused** and since when. Only the world you're in moves on; the others wait for you. A world paused in **Settings** gets **Resume**.
- Forked timelines can be **renamed** and **deleted** (with Undo), and a new one is named after the message it branched from.
- Ending a storyline also clears what it put on their mind.
- Shared outings show in both people's days, at a time of day that fits.

**Fixes**
- A late night planned before a work project changed now names the new project, and one that can't move says why.
- Background problems that stopped a world's day from moving on are now written to the log.

**If drama changed in all your worlds at once before:** open **Realism** in each world and set **Drama** once. From then on each world keeps its own.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.2.2-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.2.2-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Your picture and pictures that follow a profile picture haven't been tried on every image setup yet.** They're tested with stand-ins; results on local edit models and hosted services may vary.
- **Back when goes back to 1990 at the earliest,** and only real present-day cities take on a decade. Period and made-up cities keep their own era.
- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
