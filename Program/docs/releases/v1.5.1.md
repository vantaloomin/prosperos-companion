A follow-up to 1.5, "A Most Majestic Vision", all about Story, from the first days of playing it: you can ask for another reply or change what was said, the story is told in chapters with times and never has to be cleared to go on, a whisper stays between the two of you, the story follows you home, and Story and your companion's chat now lead to each other. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Another reply, Undo and Edit in Story**
- Under the Story's newest reply there's now **Another reply**, which tells it again. If a reply failed or never came, it says **Try again** and writes only what's missing.
- **Undo**, next to it, brings the earlier reply back, until you send your next line.
- **Edit your line** changes what you last said and has it answered anew.
- The narrator's reply or a companion's line can be reworded anywhere in the story, to cut or change something. Hover over it and choose **Edit**, or press and hold it on a phone. Everything after reads the new wording.
- A story has one course, so what a replaced reply had made people know or remember is taken back with it, and comes back with Undo.
- On a phone, a short story can now always be scrolled to its newest lines.

**Chapters and times**
- The Story is one ongoing story, told in chapters. Come back to it after a couple of hours and a new chapter starts by itself, with its number, day and time above it. **Start a new chapter** under the box starts one whenever you like.
- Nothing is cleared either way. Everything before still happened.
- Your own lines show the time they were written, on the city's own clock.
- "Start a new story" is now **Clear the pages…**, and it only removes the writing. The people you've met still know you, anyone who said yes is still a match, and time with your companions still counts.
- Exported stories are in chapters too.

**A whisper is for one person**
- What you whisper, show or slip to one person in a scene now stays with that person. Write a name on a notepad and show it only to the server, and nobody across the room remarks on it.
- Only the one it was for answers, and only they know it afterward. Say it out loud and everyone there hears it, as before.

**Story follows you home**
- Your home is now a place the story can be. **Go home** is the first choice under **Go somewhere else…**, and your home's card on the map has **Go home in your story**.
- The scene also follows what's written. "I walk home" takes the story home, and "I walk over to the Charles Theatre" takes it there, with the place at the top keeping up.
- A companion in the scene comes along, unless your line says you part ("I say goodnight and walk home").

**Story and chat lead to each other**
- After time with your companion in Story, their chat has a quiet line where it happened: "In Story at Club Charles · 2:06 to 2:31 PM", with **Read it** to open the Story at that chapter.
- While you're with them in Story, their chat says so ("With you at Club Charles, in Story") with **Back to the story**.
- In Story, a companion who isn't in the scene has **Message** beside **Go to** on their card, and when they leave, the line that says so links to their chat.
- From their chat's "…" menu, **Meet in Story** takes the story to wherever their day has them. A plan under way offers **play it out in Story** from the chat too.
- When your companion is already in the scene and nothing has been said yet, the Story opens with **Start the scene**.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.5.1-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.5.1-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **A text sent while you're together in Story is still answered as a text.** Their chat shows that you're with them, but a reply there reads as if the two of you are apart.
- **Story only follows you to places the map knows, and to your own home.** A ride somewhere, someone else's home or a place the city doesn't have isn't followed, so pick it under **Go somewhere else…**.
- **Nobody overhears a whisper yet,** whatever the Drama level.
- **Work projects don't follow where someone stands at work yet.** In a made-up world, a project can come from the wrong trade, like paperwork for someone who runs something else entirely.
- **A place called by a loose name may not be found,** so a plan goes somewhere nearby instead.
- **Dinner at home doesn't follow the city's food yet,** so a made-up city can get a dish from somewhere else.
- **A day post can still add a detail the day didn't have** (like "at the office" on a night off).
- **Something your companion already remembers from an earlier mix-up stays remembered.** If they saved a memory about a little thing that shouldn't have happened, remove it under **Memories** on their profile.
- **Finding Claude Code and Codex accounts has only been tried with stand-in command-line tools.** If an account you use isn't listed, type its folder under "Type an account folder instead".
- **Back when goes back to 1990 at the earliest,** and only real present-day cities take on a decade. Period and made-up cities keep their own era.
- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
