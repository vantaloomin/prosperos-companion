A small update to 1.1, "Such People In't", with fixes and requests from testers. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**They take a moment to read your texts**
- When your companion is free, they now take a little while to pick up the phone before they answer, like a real person would. It's shorter when you're already talking and a little sooner the closer you are, and longer when they're out and about or haven't looked at their phone in a while.
- You can keep texting while they haven't read it yet. They answer everything you sent in one reply instead of one reply per text.
- Settings > Realism > How quickly they read your texts sets the pace: At once, Quick, Natural (the default) or Slow. A companion's own Quick to reply switch, at the end of How they text, keeps them quick whatever the setting says.
- In person, on a call, out of character, or when you ask for another reply, they answer right away.

**Check for updates**
- Settings > General has a Check for updates button. It asks GitHub for the latest release and, if there's a newer one, says how to update your copy: the new installer, or `update.bat` / `update.command` for a folder copy. Nothing checks in the background.

**Pets in the circle**
- The people in your companion's circle can now have their pets changed, removed or added from their Edit card. A pet you remove stays removed.
- Fewer people have pets: about one in three on Earth. Aboard a ship or station it's about one in ten, and only pets that fit a cabin, like cats, fish, birds and hamsters. No more dogs on every deck.

**A resizable chat box**
- The message box now has a grip on its top edge, so dragging it up makes it taller. Double-click the grip to fit the text again.
- A small button folds the box to one line to see more of the chat, and the newest message no longer hides behind a growing box.

## What's fixed

- On a starship or station, Where they live in the circle said In town and Out of town. It now says Aboard the ship and Off the ship (or On the station and Off the station), and the chat follows suit.
- People added to the circle by hand all landed within a few years of your companion's age. Parents, siblings, friends and coworkers now spread out more naturally, and someone the city knows by name, like a ship's captain or engineer, gets the age and role it gives them.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.1.6-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.1.6-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **The library matches by meaning only with an embedding model.** Without one it matches on the words you use, which works but is stricter. EPUB files from the wild haven't been tried yet.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale or home Wi-Fi is new, and testers have had trouble getting a phone to load. The new checks in Settings > Phone access say what's wrong.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** the launch buttons with real installs of LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp and ComfyUI, instant local replies, looking back for older memories with real models, voice notes with real voices, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **GIPHY and pictures aboard ship** haven't been tried with a real GIPHY key or real ComfyUI pictures of the ship yet.
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
