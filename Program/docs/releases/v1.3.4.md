A fix-up update to 1.3, "A Sea-Change", from what testers ran into with plans and town news: a free afternoon named for what it holds stays that way, and the stories going around about the people in your companion's life now happen to them, in their diaries and in who they're with. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Plans that keep to what they say**
- A free block in your companion's week that's named for what it holds is that, every time. If Monday afternoons say "Bathhouse and cards", that's where they spend it, and they won't later say they spent the afternoon studying. Free time named only loosely, like "Free time" or "Seeing people", is filled the way it was.
- Things set in their plans, like a late night at work, an errand, a big day or babysitting, are never pushed out by a pastime or a salon visit. If a late night takes the start of a named afternoon, the rest of the afternoon follows it.
- Upcoming time already drawn the old way in a named block is drawn again by itself.

**Word getting around matches the world**
- Stories about the people in your companion's life only go to people they can be true for. A promotion needs someone with an employer, a breakup needs someone with a partner, and a night of comfort goes to a friend or family member who knows your companion, never a rival or an ex. A parent who works for themselves lands a big order instead of a promotion.
- What happens in a story now happens to the people in it. Their diary has a line about it, and their life changes with it: after a breakup their partner becomes an ex, a new job changes where they work, an engagement makes a fiancé. If a story is ended or turns out differently, the change is taken back.
- **Their people** lists a partner first (as a fiancé or wife when they are one) and exes last.
- Recent stories from before this update are filled in the same way. Stories already told stay told.

**Coming in 1.4.0:** more of the same. Big moments in a story will take up time in the plans of the people in it, news will be about the right person, coworkers will change when your companion changes jobs, and you'll be able to set how two people are related yourself.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.3.4-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.3.4-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **What your companion says about their people in conversation can still add things their record doesn't have.** Making that stick is planned for 1.4.0.
- **A day post can still add a detail the day didn't have** (like "at the office" on a night off).
- **Something your companion already remembers from an earlier mix-up stays remembered.** If they saved a memory about a little thing that shouldn't have happened, remove it under **Memories** on their profile.
- **A photo drawn from your companion's words has been tried on two hosted image models.** Results on local image setups may vary.
- **Finding Claude Code and Codex accounts has only been tried with stand-in command-line tools.** If an account you use isn't listed, type its folder under "Type an account folder instead".
- **Back when goes back to 1990 at the earliest,** and only real present-day cities take on a decade. Period and made-up cities keep their own era.
- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
