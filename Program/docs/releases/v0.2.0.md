Our biggest update yet. Meet new people on Matchlight, a dating app inside the Companion; get a companion started with just a name, an age and a city; and get a helper that can edit a character, a reply or a memory for you. Your companion's life also holds together better over months. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your companion and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Meet someone on Matchlight**
- Install Matchlight from the sidebar, set up a dating profile in a few quick screens, and swipe through the singles in your companion's city. They're real people from the town, plus hundreds more who are only on the app.
- A like only becomes a match if they like you back, and not everyone will. Each person has their own tastes, what they're looking for and how picky they are.
- Hit it off with a match? **Start talking to** them, and they become your companion. It's the easiest way to find someone new.
- Set your companion in an older era and you get a personal ad in the paper or the town matchmaker instead of an app.
- **Show photo** makes a picture of a match the first time you ask and keeps it.

**Getting started is easier**
- Set up your AI model first, then create your companion with just a name, an age, and where and when they live (cities are grouped by era). **More options** has the rest.
- Already have a character written somewhere? Paste it, or drop in a character card, and the Companion sorts it into the right fields.
- Life details like schedule, money, home and wardrobe are drafted for you and tucked behind **Show details** until you want them.

**The helper sidecar**
- A helper panel sits beside the app wherever you are. It can see your companion's character, your recent chat and their memories, and it stays outside the conversation, so your companion never sees it.
- Ask it to rework a character field, reword one of your companion's replies, or fix a memory. It shows each change before and after, and you choose to apply it, dismiss it, or undo it later.

**Your companion knows what's going on**
- Turn on **A sense of what's going on** in Settings and, once a day, your companion picks up what movies, shows, songs, books and trends people are talking about. They can bring it up themselves and post about it, but won't pretend to have seen or heard something they haven't. Nothing about you is sent. It's off until you switch it on, and only applies to companions living in the present day.

**A profile for your companion**
- Your companion's page is now a profile with their picture, bio, home town, local time and interests, with tabs for **Messages**, **Posts** and **Character**. It follows the chat style you've picked.

**A life that holds together**
- Your companion has a real wardrobe that fits their pay, job and personality. Clothes wear out, get replaced or are bought new over time, and their photos show what they're actually wearing.
- What your companion says about their own life (a sister's name, a team they play on, where they work) is remembered and stays the same months later.
- Things that happen to your companion are told as their news, not handed back to you as if they happened to you.
- They're less likely to fall back on the same pet phrases.

**Fixing things that are wrong**
- Tell your companion "Your sister is Ashley, not Jo" or "you don't work there" and the correction sticks. The old version stops coming back in later chats.
- When something your companion says doesn't match their character, it waits in Character Studio for you to decide which is right instead of quietly becoming true.
- Memories you've said are wrong are marked "You said this was wrong."

**Memory is on by default**
- Your companion now remembers automatically, including personal details, unless you turn it off in Settings. Corrections and contradictions still wait for you.

**Townsfolk and names**
- Want a different crowd? **Seed new townsfolk** under Character > Townsfolk fills the same places with different people.
- Names fit the time and place much better, with about a hundred names per decade for every culture and era, and no more made-up names.

**Story mode (optional)**
- Turn on **Story mode** in Settings > Advanced to get a narrator who plays out your own story around the cities: where you are, the weather, who's around. Your companion never sees it. You can keep a list of the people you meet and switch to one of them as your main character. With Story mode on, you can also go on dates with your Matchlight matches.

**Pictures**
- Krea 2 pictures look more like real photos, with the right outfit, what your companion is actually doing and the light for that time of day. No more mismatched shoes.
- Each image backend can have its own **Prompt style** in Settings > Images (say, an anime style for NovelAI and a photo style for Krea). Leave it empty to use the general style.
- Using OpenRouter or another OpenAI-compatible image service that allows adult content? Its backend has an **Also take NSFW requests** switch, off by default. Google, the OpenAI API and Codex stay safe-only, and the Companion's own safety check still runs first.
- For ComfyUI users: pick model files straight from your server in Settings > Images, with Krea 2 files listed first. You can also add up to three style LoRAs and change the sampler settings.

**Models**
- Signed in with ChatGPT through Codex? **Test connection** now lists the models your plan can use, so you can pick one instead of typing its name.
- Memory search (recall) now has its own section in Settings > Models, so it can use a different service from your chat model. Your current setup carries over.
- **Duplicate** copies a model profile, handy for trying a variation without retyping everything.

**How many companions can I have?**
- We put it to the test: a recent computer handles 100+ companions easily. An older laptop (about a 2012 Core i3) is comfortable up to about 50, and 100 still works.

## What's fixed

- The Posts tab no longer fails with "The request could not be completed" after using Debug time, and the profile has a **Message** button to get back to the chat.
- The Home City picker no longer shows only "None" when one of your own saved cities can't be read. That city is skipped and listed under Settings > Cities, where you can save it as a file or delete it.
- `update.bat` and `update.command` now work on a copy downloaded as a ZIP, instead of failing because it isn't a Git clone.
- Replies no longer come back blank when a model spends all its room thinking. The Companion asks again with more room.
- Your companion knows what their own day holds, so they won't say they're at work on their day off.
- Birthday wishes arrive even if you didn't answer their last text, and they never send the same first message twice.
- Replies can start faster on local models and cost less on paid ones, because the start of what's sent stays the same from reply to reply.
- Hovering over a message no longer makes the chat jump. On touch screens the ⋯ menu floats over the chat and closes after you pick something.
- The Retro IM switches in Settings only show up when the Retro IM chat style is picked.
- Panels stacked down a page, like in Memories and Story, no longer touch each other.
- The drama slider's description is shorter.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-0.2.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-0.2.0-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

Your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it.

## Known issues

- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** phone access over Tailscale, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
