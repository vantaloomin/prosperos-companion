**Someone with a life of their own.** The first public release of Prospero's Companion: a companion who lives real days in a real city, with friends, plans and news to share, running privately on your own PC with the models you choose. This is an early beta.

## What's in it

- **A life of their own.** A weekly routine in their city, a hidden week-ahead plan for them and everyone they know, plans from chat that actually happen, weather, holidays, birthdays, money and budgets, a home that changes, a body that gets tired or sick, and storylines with a drama slider. Built from data and templates on your PC; the model only phrases it.
- **People around them.** Family, friends, coworkers, friends of friends and townsfolk met around town, a private social Feed, and the option to make someone they've met the main character.
- **They text first.** Check-ins on breaks, follow-ups, news and photos, within daily limits and quiet hours. Availability is never shown; a busy companion answers later.
- **They stay in character.** No AI disclaimers; `OOC:` or ((double parentheses)) for a plain answer.
- **Memory you control.** Opt-in automatic capture, a Memories page showing exactly what the next reply uses, approval for sensitive facts, closeness stages.
- **Pictures.** Selfies, photos, views and memes through the Codex CLI, a local ComfyUI or hosted APIs, classified on your PC first (NSFW only to local ComfyUI, prohibited content refused everywhere).
- **Cities.** Real (Baltimore, New York, Miami, San Diego, Las Vegas), public-domain (Victorian London, Camelot, Oz) and original cities, plus your own.
- **Real-world context.** Weather for both of you, holidays and seasons, optional Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups.
- **Your choice of model.** OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, any OpenAI-compatible service, your Codex login, or a local model through LM Studio or Kobold, with a different model per job if you like.
- **Everything else.** Guided character creation, opt-in emotional edges, five chat styles, branching timelines, import from Prospero's Study, backups and restore, phone access over Tailscale, and Debug time for testing.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-0.1.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only, needs no administrator rights, Python or Node, and adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-0.1.0-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. A Git clone can update itself later with `update.bat`.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time; [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

Your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it.

## Known issues

- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** phone access over Tailscale, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend; Krea 2 workflows for ComfyUI haven't been verified on real hardware.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
