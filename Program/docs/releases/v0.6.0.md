A life for every you. You can now be more than one person in the Companion: each persona you make gets worlds of their own, with their own companions, town and history, even in the same city. Bring your characters over from other apps, launch your local AI programs from Settings, and come back to a catch-up of what happened while you were away. Your worlds are also backed up automatically. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Everything you already have becomes your first world, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Worlds and personas**
- A persona is you as someone in particular, with a name, gender, age and a little about you. Each persona has one or more worlds, and each world has its own companions, chats, memories, townsfolk and life going on. You can even have two worlds in the same city.
- Switching persona or world takes the whole app there. Your settings, models and cities come along.
- A new world is ready right away, with fresh townsfolk and a starter companion picked from them.
- Upgrading turns everything you already have into your first world, with a persona made from what the app already knows about you.

**Bring your characters**
- Have characters from SillyTavern, Chub, Agnai, RisuAI, Backyard, Pygmalion or NovelAI? Import a character card (JSON, PNG, CHARX and more) and they become a companion living in this world in one step. The town gives them a job, a home and a routine.
- Lorebooks come along too, from the card itself or from SillyTavern, NovelAI, Agnai and RisuAI lorebook files. Always-on entries are always known, and keyword entries come up when they're mentioned.

**While you were away**
- Come back after a few days away and the chat opens with a short catch-up of what happened in their life and around town. It needs no AI calls. Settings > Life sets how many days count as away, or turns it off.

**Launch your local models**
- Start LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp or ComfyUI from Settings > Models > Local programs, from a model profile or from the ComfyUI image setting, using the address and model you already set. The Companion finds the usual install locations and only asks for a path when it can't.
- Turn on **Start these when the Companion starts** and they open with the app. It's off by default.

**Instant local replies**
- With llama-server started with `--slot-save-path`, each companion's conversation is saved and put back when you switch, so coming back to someone answers right away instead of re-reading the whole chat.

**Automatic backups**
- Every world is backed up automatically, once a day by default, keeping the last 7 daily and 4 weekly backups.

**Remember this on their replies**
- **Remember this** now works on your companion's replies too, so they hold on to something they said about themselves, like a new job or a favorite band. It's kept as their own fact, never as a memory about you.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-0.6.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-0.6.0-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale or home Wi-Fi is new, and testers have had trouble getting a phone to load. The new checks in Settings > Phone access say what's wrong.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** the launch buttons with real installs of LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp and ComfyUI, instant local replies, voice notes with real voices, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
