Life together. Relationships now move. A romance goes from dating to exclusive, to the first "I love you", to meeting their people, to a key to each other's place, to moving in, and maybe to a proposal and a wedding. A friendship can grow close, and now and then a friend falls for you. There are rough patches and making up, and on Real life and Drama, breaks and breakups too. Your companions also have love lives of their own. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Next steps**
- Every relationship has steps. A romance runs from dating, being exclusive and the first "I love you" through meeting their people and a key to their place. A friendship runs through best friends and a first trip together.
- Either of you can bring up the next step. They ask when the time feels right, and your answer in the chat is the answer. Say "I love you" or "we should move in together" yourself and it counts.
- Now and then a close friend falls for you. Set any friend to **Just friends** to keep it that way.
- Each step becomes an anniversary and a scrapbook line. Their Character page shows where things stand, and you can set it yourself.

**Meeting their people**
- At that step they invite you to dinner with their family, or drinks with their friends if their family lives elsewhere.
- Everyone there forms a first impression of you, which changes as you keep plans, have rough patches or call things off. Your companion tells you what they thought, and people who've met you remember you.

**Moving in together**
- When you decide to live together, the app picks whose place it'll be (or who moves cities), sets a moving day, and splits the rent and the chores. Whoever didn't cook does the dishes.
- Evenings at home are spent together, with a strip for heading out and coming home.
- If you're seeing more than one companion, everyone is asked before a second partner moves in. If someone isn't ready, the move waits.

**Rough patches and making up**
- Hurtful words, a missed plan, a forgotten birthday, a broken promise or a plan called off on the day can start a rough patch, and either of you can be at fault. Companions can stand you up too, and then apologize first.
- While it lasts they hold back on texts, invitations and calls. It mends with an apology, a kind gesture, a plan that goes well or a few calm days.
- Their Character page says "Things are a bit tense" and what it's about.

**Breaks and breakups**
- On Real life and Drama, rough patches that drag on can strain things. You'll see the warnings first ("Things are strained", then "They're thinking about a break"), and you can talk them round at every step.
- A break ends with trying again or ending things, and a friendship can fall out instead. You can end it yourself from the chat too.
- Exes keep their own lives, rarely text, and may reach out about trying again. Nothing is deleted. Turn breakups off with the Breaks and breakups switch. They never happen on Easy life.

**Their own love life**
- A friend can meet someone, and the two of them move along their own steps, splits included.
- A romance only sees others in an open relationship, or in secret on Drama, where it can come out through friends or the town paper. When it does, they own up and apologize.
- Townsfolk couples get together too, and the town paper reports their engagements and weddings.

**Engagement and wedding**
- Once you live together, your companion may propose on an evening out at a favorite place, with a ring they paid for.
- Say yes and plan the wedding on their Character page: size, day, venue, budget, and guests from both your circles, following your city's customs.
- The wedding is a big day out with guests, a photo, a page in your year and a notice in the town paper.

**Clearer odds**
- Under Hidden values, the reasons behind each outcome are now grouped as "More likely because…" and "Less likely because…".

**More Realism settings**
- Settings > Realism has new Relationship pace, Rough patches and Their love life sliders, and Friends can fall for you and Breaks and breakups switches. The presets set them for you, and older worlds get the values of their preset.

## What's fixed

- Telling a companion "go away for the weekend" or "my stupid phone" no longer hurts their feelings.
- Reasons for a called-off plan fit the city's era, so nobody in Camelot says their car wouldn't start.
- A friend who moves away moves to a city of their own time.
- Memories of visits and outings are worded the right way round for you and your companion.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-0.13.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-0.13.0-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale or home Wi-Fi is new, and testers have had trouble getting a phone to load. The new checks in Settings > Phone access say what's wrong.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** the launch buttons with real installs of LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp and ComfyUI, instant local replies, looking back for older memories with real models, voice notes with real voices, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
