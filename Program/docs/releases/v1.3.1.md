A fix-up update to 1.3, "A Sea-Change", built from what testers ran into in its first day: what your companion remembers, money in city packs, work and pay, pausing worlds and pictures. It also lets you drop by unannounced. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Drop by unannounced**
- Your companion's home and workplace on the **Map** now have **Drop by**. You go over without asking, and how it goes depends on their day: they let you in and you're there together, nobody's home (with where they are and until when), the lights are off because they're asleep, or it's not a good time.
- At their work they keep working while you're there, so it's a short visit. The card says whether they're on shift and until when.

**Memories that are worth keeping**
- Your companion saves fewer throwaway things: no more "Likes: it a lot", half sentences saved as a look, or what family calls someone ("Ma") saved as their name.
- Plans are only saved when they have a day or an event, so last week's meals, chores and "maybe next time" ideas no longer fill up **Plans**.
- A plan closes the day after its day comes, and one with no day closes after a week of not coming up, instead of two.

**Money and work**
- A city pack written without a currency or rents now gets them by rule: a currency from the city's own words or its era, and rents borrowed from the closest built-in city. Today no longer says there's no money there.
- A modern city in a pack outside the US pays in its own country's money, at that money's usual sizes.
- A world's own coin is said by name ("28 gold") instead of a symbol that could read as something else.
- On the Character page, **Work and money** has **Something else (write it in)** for work the list doesn't have, and a **Pay** box to set exactly what they take home. Left empty, the pay is worked out as before.

**Pausing worlds**
- Pausing one world no longer pauses the next world you go to, and resuming no longer resumes the one you left.
- A world you've left isn't marked **Paused** any more, since it catches up on the time away when you go back. Only a world you paused yourself says so.
- Every world's menu on the **Worlds** page has **Pause** and **Resume**.

**Pictures**
- Their looks are said once in a picture prompt, not twice, so a feature isn't overdone.
- A picture of an errand or paperwork shows the things it was about, without anyone in it.
- **Settings > Advanced > Prompts** has a new **Pictures** group where you can reword the negative prompt, how a picture opens, camera details, the face for each mood (and add moods of your own), and what each activity looks like. Until you change them, pictures come out as before.

**Fixes**
- The step words under the **Realism** sliders ("True to life" and so on) open their explanation on hover, click, tap or the keyboard, on a phone too.
- Asking your companion whether they remember the nickname they call you no longer starts a phone call.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.3.1-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.3.1-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Finding Claude Code and Codex accounts has only been tried with stand-in command-line tools.** If an account you use isn't listed, type its folder under "Type an account folder instead".
- **Your picture and pictures that follow a profile picture haven't been tried on every image setup yet.** They're tested with stand-ins; results on local edit models and hosted services may vary.
- **Back when goes back to 1990 at the earliest,** and only real present-day cities take on a decade. Period and made-up cities keep their own era.
- **Cities you write in the app or bring from a world map still have no money of their own.** Today's budget works in built-in cities and city packs for now.
- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
