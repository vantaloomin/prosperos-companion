"This is a most majestic vision, and harmonious charmingly." Prospero's Companion 1.5 brings you into your companion's world. Things that happen in their life now come to include you: a friend who wants to meet you, a favor, a place they want to show you, and what you do with it changes how it goes. Story grows into a full part of every world: your companion is there in the scene with you, what happens there counts and is remembered, and you can play out a plan or ask someone out. Companions who run their own place, or manage people, are treated that way at work. It folds in every fix planned for 1.4.2 as well. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Things that involve you**
- Your companion's life now has storylines you're part of: a friend who wants to meet you, a favor they need, a place they want to show you, trouble at their work, someone who complicates things, the goal they're saving for, and the two of you growing closer. There are 110 of them.
- A storyline reaches you the way life would: a text from your companion, or an invitation that books a plan on Today.
- Nothing waits for you. Answer, say yes or no, go, or let it pass, and each leads somewhere different. If you skip a plan, they may go anyway, and their day shows it.
- Your companion knows each turn the story took and what part you played, so chat keeps up with it.
- Today has a new **Things that involve you** section above what's going on in their world. A plan that's part of one says so on its card, with **See it**. A finished storyline stays on Today for a week, then is kept in the scrapbook under **Things you were part of**.
- A new **Things that involve you** slider in Settings > Realism: Off, Now and then, True to life (about once a week, where it starts) or Often. How big they get follows the Drama level, and you can end any of them on Today.
- The storylines in your companion's own life and their circle's grew from nineteen to over a hundred: more good news for a quiet world, more everyday trouble at Realistic, and more to go wrong at Dramatic and Soap opera. New ones are about a sibling, a grandparent, a neighbor, any friend, and your companion's own days outside work.

**Story, in every world**
- Story is no longer tucked away in Settings > Advanced. It's a tab in every world. A story you haven't begun opens with **Look around**, and on the map a place offers **Go there in your story**.
- **Your companion is in the scene.** Companions turn up where their own day has them: at work during a shift, out where their plans take them, and not at all while they're asleep or unwell. They speak for themselves after the narrator, and come and go as their day goes on.
- **Scenes count.** Time with them in Story is part of your life together. A day spent with them there counts toward Closeness, a real conversation leaves a shared moment in your history, and a step you take in person is real. Back in chat, they remember what was said. Turn off **Story scenes count** in Settings > Realism to keep Story apart.
- **What's said sticks.** What you tell them in a scene becomes a memory ("Said in Story"), and so do the things they say about themselves. A scene can lift their mood or sour it, the way a chat does.
- **Secrets stay secrets.** When others are in the scene, everyone there hears what's said, and your companion keeps to themselves what they shouldn't share. At Soap opera, a secret might slip, and then everyone there knows it.
- **Play a plan as a scene.** While a plan with your companion is under way somewhere in town, Today's Out together card offers to **play it out in Story**. Friends along for the plan are in the scene too.
- **Ask someone out.** Charming someone in a scene now leads somewhere. Ask them out in your own words, or with "Ask Dana out" on their card under People you've met. Their own life answers: someone who's seeing someone says so, someone who isn't drawn to you says no, and anyone else is likelier to say yes the more days you've come by. A yes can become a companion with **Start talking**, and shows in Matchlight as "Met in your story". No dating profile is needed.

**Where they stand at work**
- The app now works out where a companion stands at work from their card and their career: **Runs the place**, **Manages people** or **Works for someone**.
- Whoever runs the place no longer gets a yearly review, a promotion, layoffs or a new boss. They get stories of their own instead: a slow stretch, a supplier putting prices up, a rival opening nearby, a record week, an offer to buy the place, and once a year a good year or a flat one. Someone who manages people gets stories from the middle: told to cut the team, a hard review to give, a target hit. A business your companion opened in one of Life's big turns gets these stories too.
- Chat knows it too. A companion who runs the place says how long they've run it, and asked who they answer to, no longer makes up a boss.
- Work and money on the character form has a **Position** picker beside Hours. Left on Picked for them, it shows what the app read.

**Fixes**
- **Plans on the day you agreed:** a plan your companion suggests no longer lands a week or more after the day they named. "Friday" said on a Friday means today, and in "Tuesday's a mess, want to walk Friday at 4?" the walk is on Friday.
- **Finished work stays finished:** when your companion says their project at work is done, it's done, and the next one starts the day after. Choosing another version of that reply brings it back. A project you wrote yourself, or gave a due date, stays until you change it.
- **Trips from what was said:** a trip away together is only booked from what the two of you said, on the day you named. One your companion plans as their own idea shows once they've told you about it. **Never planned** on a trip takes one off quietly if it shouldn't be there, and you can undo it.
- **Their own plans:** a promise to come and find you is no longer saved as a plan of their own, and a post about a plan says the plan in their words instead of a bare "As planned."
- **Pictures that match the moment:** a post's picture follows where they were and when. Lunch out isn't at home, an afternoon post has afternoon light, and rain shows through the window when they're in.
- **Who knows you:** in their circle, the people who've met you, or heard about you from your companion, are linked to you. Each person's card has a **You** row with the reason, which you can change.
- **Meeting people around town:** a companion who mostly works and stays in now meets people too, over lunch out, on the way to and from work, and at their workplace. After a few quiet days a meeting is sure to come, still at most one a day.
- **Story keeps your companion's clock:** when your companion's time zone isn't their city's, the Story in their city now runs on their clock, so a scene isn't at midnight while their day has them out.
- **No made-up remote job:** a companion whose time zone was set to yours is no longer told they work remotely for a team in another time zone.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.5.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.5.0-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Work projects don't follow where someone stands at work yet.** In a made-up world, a project can come from the wrong trade, like paperwork for someone who runs something else entirely.
- **A place called by a loose name may not be found,** so a plan goes somewhere nearby instead.
- **Dinner at home doesn't follow the city's food yet,** so a made-up city can get a dish from somewhere else.
- **A day post can still add a detail the day didn't have** (like "at the office" on a night off).
- **Something your companion already remembers from an earlier mix-up stays remembered.** If they saved a memory about a little thing that shouldn't have happened, remove it under **Memories** on their profile.
- **Finding Claude Code and Codex accounts has only been tried with stand-in command-line tools.** If an account you use isn't listed, type its folder under "Type an account folder instead".
- **Back when goes back to 1990 at the earliest,** and only real present-day cities take on a decade. Period and made-up cities keep their own era.
- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
