A fix-up update to 1.4, "Another Storm Brewing", from what testers ran into in the first days: someone in the hospital stays there as long as it fits, a place you name joins the map, Today's outing card keeps up with the scene, a trip you agree on goes where and when you said, and a reply that names a time no longer fails. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Places you name join the map**
- Ask "Dinner tonight at 7 at Woodberry Kitchen?" in a city that doesn't have Woodberry Kitchen, and it's added to the map and the outing happens there, instead of being swapped for a place nearby. The app works out what kind of place it is, which neighborhood it's in, and its hours and prices from places like it.
- The place stays in that world, so your companion can go back, and it shows up around town and in Story mode.
- Its card on the map lets you change the name, kind, neighborhood or description, or remove it (with **Undo**).

**Away together goes where you agreed**
- A trip agreed in chat keeps the place and the day you said. Ask for "a trip by the river on Monday" and that's where and when it goes.
- A walk, a coffee or a dinner on one day stays an outing, instead of turning into a weekend away.
- **Change place** on the trip card now lists the places those days can reach and they can afford, with the first plan always offered back, instead of cycling through them one at a time.

**Hospital stays that fit the cause**
- When your companion says someone is in the hospital or unwell, that person is out of their usual days for as long as what was said suggests: a night, a few days, a few weeks, or until someone says they're home. "Right now" no longer shows them at work that evening.
- Saying they've recovered, or **Undo** on the diary line, ends it early.

**Today's Out together card follows the scene**
- On a date or meetup, the card on Today now shows where the chat took you, like the Where strip does: the new spot, where you came from, and **Back to** if it read something wrong. Planned and finished outings keep the plan as it was.

**Celsius or Fahrenheit**
- Temperatures now come in the city's own unit: °F in the United States, °C elsewhere. That goes for Today's weather line, the town paper and what your companion says.
- **Settings > General > Temperature** sets one unit for every world if you'd rather.

**A town paper worth reading**
- The Sunday paper runs one goal story an issue, not three. It only prints wins people would share, so nobody's bird list or rainy-day fund makes the news, and it says how long the goal really took ("about two months", "about a year"). The wording changes from story to story.

**Characters from Prospero's Dramatis arrive as written**
- A card exported from Dramatis for the Prospero apps now comes in word for word under **Bring your characters**: their routine, week, texting habits, emotional edges and personality as you set them there. The town still gives them a home: their city, neighborhood, clock and money. A card from a Dramatis this version can't read comes in the old way.

**Fixes**
- **Replies that name a time:** asking something like "what did I tell you last Monday?" could make the reply fail. It answers now.
- **Their own words and your name:** a companion who writes in all lowercase could have your name, your nickname or the names of people and places they know caught as a repeated phrase. Names are never caught now, however they're written.
- **The home card when they own it:** after you set a home as owned under **Home** in their Money details, the Home card could still say "Rent about 150 a month". The card, the chat and the lease errand now follow what you set, and an owned home shows its upkeep instead.
- **Week lines that sound like your week:** the history's week lines could pick odd moments ("Mom's calls. Trust in assistant.") and a shopping line as the quote. They now pick outings, firsts and dates first, call your companion by name, and quote what says the most about the two of you.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.4.1-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.4.1-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **A day post can still add a detail the day didn't have** (like "at the office" on a night off).
- **Something your companion already remembers from an earlier mix-up stays remembered.** If they saved a memory about a little thing that shouldn't have happened, remove it under **Memories** on their profile.
- **Finding Claude Code and Codex accounts has only been tried with stand-in command-line tools.** If an account you use isn't listed, type its folder under "Type an account folder instead".
- **Back when goes back to 1990 at the earliest,** and only real present-day cities take on a decade. Period and made-up cities keep their own era.
- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
