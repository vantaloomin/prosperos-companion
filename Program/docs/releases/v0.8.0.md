Out on the town. Your companions' city is no longer something you only read about: open a map and see where they live, where they go and where they are right now, look at a web of everyone they know, and check their status line to see what they're up to. Ask them out and it really happens, they take trips and send postcards, their families keep their holidays, and a scrapbook looks back on your year together. The town got bigger too, with eight new cities, a Jazz Age era, a fantasy port city and townsfolk who each have a past. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Maps**
- Open a map from Today, Story or any **Show on map** link. It shows your companion's home, their usual places, where they've been lately and where a Story scene is happening.
- Tap any place for a card that shows who goes there and when they were last there. **Suggest going together** puts an invitation in your message box for you to send or change, and with Story mode on, **Go there** takes the scene there.
- Real cities get a street map. Fictional, past-era and your own cities get a drawn map in the same style, with districts, side streets, main roads, parks and any sea or rivers the city has.

**Who knows who**
- A web of everyone you've met or heard about: you, your companions, their families and friends, people around town and your Matchlight matches. Drag it around, search it, or filter it to one companion's close circle or one neighbourhood.
- Prefer a list? The List view shows the same people and connections, and works with a keyboard and screen readers.

**Status messages**
- Every companion now has a status line, like an old AIM away message, written from what's going on in their life: "payday 💸", "plans Saturday 🗓️", "home sick 🤒", "currently: pottery".
- While they're asleep, at work or out, a short away line shows instead, like "at work till 6" or "zzz". It's only a hint, and you can still text them anytime. There are still no online dots, read receipts or typing bubbles.
- Click a status on their profile card to write your own. It stays until something big happens in their life.

**Spots inside places**
- Places and homes now have spots of their own, so things happen somewhere specific: "at the corner table by the window", "out on the back patio", "in the jukebox corner". You'll see them in their days and in Story scenes.

**Go somewhere together**
- Ask a companion out ("Want to get dinner Friday?") and it becomes a real plan. They pick a real place in their city that suits their taste, the season and their budget, and it goes in their calendar.
- On the night, the chat sets the scene: where you're sitting, what's on the menu and what it costs, the weather. A strip above the message box lets you **Head home** when you're done. Afterwards it's a shared memory, a photo on their feed if pictures are on, and the bill comes out of their budget.
- Today lists your plans under **Out together**, with **Somewhere else**, **Call it off** and **Head home**, and each one can be undone.

**Trips and postcards**
- Every few weeks a companion may go away for a weekend or a long weekend to another real city of the same era, to see family who live there, with a friend or on their own.
- Their days fill with places there, they send you a postcard picture on the first afternoon, the trip shows in their budget, and they might come home sunburnt. Today shows trips under **Trips**.

**Family traditions**
- Every companion's family has a few holidays of their own: "Thanksgiving is always at their mom Valerie's in Hampden. There's always green bean casserole with the crispy onions."
- Traditions come up in chat and on Today the week before, the day itself goes to them, and decorations go up and come down at home with the seasons.
- You can reword, drop, bring back or add traditions on the Character page.

**Our year so far**
- A scrapbook of your year together, opened from Memories (and from Today on an anniversary or at New Year). Page through the first thing you said, pictures they sent, how you got closer, running jokes, shared moments, their own year and a closing line.

**Eight new cities, and the Jazz Age**
- New cities: Chicago, New Orleans, London today, Jeju (South Korea), Osaka (Japan) and New York in 1925. There are also two originals: **Haddon Harbor**, a cozy Maine harbour town where everyone knows everyone, and **Pellmouth**, a 1926 Massachusetts river town with a cosmic-horror lean, where the strange stays rumour and legend.
- A new **Jazz Age** era brings 1920s jobs, holidays, money and wardrobe, plus a personal column in the paper for dating.
- Local holidays come with them, including Mardi Gras in New Orleans and the lunar holidays in Jeju and Osaka. Prices show in pounds, won and yen where they belong.
- Townsfolk fit their town: Pellmouth has quarry workers and asylum attendants rather than stockbrokers, and a soda jerk works only where there's a soda fountain.

**Grandport**
- Grandport, the merchant republic's capital from Oak's dark fantasy world Neokosmos, is now a built-in city, included with Oak's permission. Nine of the setting's own characters live there, and your companion can run into them around town.
- Any city pack can now place its own named characters in town too.

**Featured and sorted cities**
- Settings > Cities sorts every city into Real / Modern, Other Eras, Fictional and Custom, with a search box. City pickers use the same groups.
- Each release now puts a few cities up front. This time it's an old favourite, Baltimore, and three new places to call home: Grandport, Pellmouth and Osaka.

**Townsfolk with a past**
- Everyone in town has a life story now: where they come from, the jobs they've had, family, sometimes a pet, things they like and stories from their past. Your companion learns it one meeting at a time, and it shows in chats, the diary, Around town and Story mode.
- Becoming a townsperson brings their whole story with them.
- Because the lists grew, people you've already met may come across a little differently. Nothing is lost.

**Looks**
- Every companion and townsperson now has a full looks sheet covering age, height, weight, build, skin tone, face, eyes, hair and one feature people notice. Pictures use it, so the same person shows up every time.
- Fill in or change any of it under **Show details > Looks** on the character form. Anything you leave empty is picked for you, within believable limits.
- Ask your companion how tall they are, and they'll answer the way their pictures show them.

**A simpler phone layout**
- On a phone, the bottom bar now has five clear tabs: Chats, Today, Story (while Story mode is on), Matchlight and Settings. Chats holds your one-on-one and group chats together, Memories moved into each profile, and the sidecar opens from the chat header.

**Adult side of life (off unless you turn it on)**
- A new switch in Settings > Realism gives adult townsfolk and companions an orientation, how adventurous they are and a few interests, for stories between consenting adults. It's off by default, it never applies to anyone under 18, and it only shapes their own chat, never the feed, the paper or pictures.

## What's fixed

- Everything in v0.7.1 is included.
- Today no longer lists the same plan twice.
- Story scenes no longer repeat the same unnamed local over and over.
- Phone chats no longer show blank gaps between messages, in all five chat styles.
- In a narrow window, a message's buttons sit in one small row on its top edge instead of pushing the chat around, in every chat style.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-0.8.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-0.8.0-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale or home Wi-Fi is new, and testers have had trouble getting a phone to load. The new checks in Settings > Phone access say what's wrong.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** the launch buttons with real installs of LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp and ComfyUI, instant local replies, looking back for older memories with real models, voice notes with real voices, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates. Neighbourhoods on drawn maps aren't to scale, and the map says so.
