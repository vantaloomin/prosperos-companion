
A small update to 1.1, "Such People In't", with requests from testers and fixes found before they reached you. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Families that look like real families**
- A new companion's family is now planned on its own, at true-to-life rates for their city's era. Parents can be together, divorced, remarried or on their own, and there can be stepparents, half siblings, stepsiblings, adopted children, two moms or two dads, only children, a parent or sibling who died years ago, and family they no longer talk to.
- What you write about them comes first. "Only child", "late mother" or "raised by two dads" is followed, and so are the parents and siblings you name.
- Today > People is grouped into Family, Everyone else, Not in touch and Remembered. **Restore** brings anyone back, alive and in touch.
- Settings > Realism has a new **Family shapes** slider: Simple, Gentle, True to life (the default) and More drama.
- Companions you already have keep the family they have.

**One saved key for everything**
- A key you saved once for a service is now used by every profile on that same service, so setting up recall or a second chat model no longer asks for it again. The form says which profile the key comes from, and **Use a different key** is there if you want another one.
- Image services with no key of their own use the key saved for the same service's text models. A key never goes to a different service or address.

**Switch models fast, or mix them**
- **Add another model** on a profile copies it with the same key and settings, ready for a different model.
- Once you have two or more chat models ready, the chat's More menu has a **Model** choice to switch on the spot. If a reply fails, **Try with** another model sits next to Retry.
- **Mix models in chat** (Settings > Models, off by default) lets several models take turns answering, in order or at random, each with its own settings.

**Fixes from testers**
- **Name styles fit who they are:** a gruff pit fighter no longer gets a pink handwritten name. Name styles now lean on who the companion is, and there's a new Bold condensed style for fighters, soldiers and athletes.
- **Work in made-up worlds:** in medieval, fantasy and other made-up cities, Character > Work now lists the city's own jobs, and the first choice says what the app would guess for them.
- **Books, shows and songs from their own world:** a companion in a made-up world now reads, watches and hums titles from that world, built from its own places, people, festivals and lore, instead of the same generic titles everywhere. A city file can also list its own.
  - If you use a city pack that now comes with its own books, shows and songs, copy its updated city files into your `city-packs` folder again (replacing the old ones), then press **Reload packs** in Settings > Life & cities or restart the app.
- **Famous people around town:** a city's well-known people now stand out when your companion is out and about, so you'll actually meet them, and new townsfolk keep the ones your companion already knows.
- **Notifications:** opening the app from a notification no longer leaves the newest message blank until you hover over it, and a message you already read isn't announced later.
- **Several tabs:** when you switch worlds in one tab or on your phone, other tabs follow right away and say where they went.
- **Group names on the phone:** the message box says the whole group name ("Message Night out"), and long names end neatly in the header and chat list.
- **Strangers aren't friends from day one:** someone you haven't met yet starts at Just met and reads as "Getting to know each other" until you are actually friends.
- **Pastimes you add** read as something they've done for years, not "just started".
- **No more old typing in text boxes:** the browser no longer offers words you typed elsewhere, like the stray words that turned up in Also into.

**Other fixes**
- Adding a mom, dad or spouse by hand no longer leaves the app-made one there as well. The app-made one steps aside and comes back if you remove yours.
- Medieval and fantasy cities written without careers of their own now have jobs and their own money (silver pennies, or shillings in Victorian and steampunk cities), and young people without work no longer read as retired.
- Aboard ship, pets, clothes and posts fit ship life, and posts in older eras no longer mention tacos, TV or iced coffee. Before today's era, takeout reads as a cooked supper picked up.
- Tips and the chat's More menu stay on screen on a phone.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.1.8-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.1.8-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **The library matches by meaning only with an embedding model.** Without one it matches on the words you use, which works but is stricter. EPUB files from the wild haven't been tried yet.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale or home Wi-Fi is new, and testers have had trouble getting a phone to load. The new checks in Settings > Phone access say what's wrong.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** the launch buttons with real installs of LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp and ComfyUI, instant local replies, looking back for older memories with real models, voice notes with real voices, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **GIPHY and pictures aboard ship** haven't been tried with a real GIPHY key or real ComfyUI pictures of the ship yet.
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
