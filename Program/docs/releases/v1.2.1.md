A small update to 1.2, "The Dark Backward", that tidies what your companion remembers and fixes a few things testers ran into. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Tell them more about your people**
- Each person under **Memories > People in your life** now has **Tell (your companion) more**. Add a free note about them, or something on a topic: their work, where they live, age, birthday, studies, family, likes, dislikes or news. Your companion knows it from then on, just like something you said in chat.
- People are shown one card each, like the people in your companion's circle, with what's known about them at a glance and **Tell more**, **Edit details**, **Rename** and **Forget** underneath. Pets get pet suggestions and aren't asked about work, studies or family.

**Plans that are over close on their own**
- Plans and promises no longer pile up in Memories forever. Once a plan's day has passed (a few days more if the day was only a guess), or a plan with no date hasn't come up for two weeks, it's taken as done.
- Your companion treats it as probably done, not certain, so they can still ask how it went or bring up a plan that fell through.
- Done and cancelled plans fold away under **Done and cancelled**. One closed on its own is marked **Assumed done** and offers **It happened**, **It didn't happen** and **It moved**, so you can set it straight or give it a new date.

**Work that fits the season and the job**
- Work projects tied to a time of year, like a midsummer festival rush or a Christmas window, only come up when they're due, not in October.
- A job the city doesn't list is read from who they are, so someone who runs the fighting pits gets projects for the ring, not guild orders. A project the app picked that no longer fits the season or the job is swapped for one that does, unless you set its title or due date yourself.

**Fixes**
- **Promised photos:** your companion no longer says a photo is on its way ("resending now") when none is being sent. Asking where a promised picture is now sends one with the reply.
- **Small talk stays small talk:** chatting about the world in general, like what bats eat, was sometimes saved as memories about someone called "Bats". Only things about you and the people in your life are kept now, and the app quietly clears out the strays it saved before. Memories you pinned or edited stay put.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.2.1-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.2.1-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Back when goes back to 1990 at the earliest,** and only real present-day cities take on a decade. Period and made-up cities keep their own era.
- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
