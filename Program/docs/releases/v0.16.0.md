Such stuff as dreams. This is the last feature release before 1.0. You can now sit back and just watch a world go on without you, following anyone in town through their day. Cities can be ships and stations, and a built-in generation ship, The Long Meridian, sails with its station Port Vesper. Companions send memes and reaction GIFs even without a picture model, and children now have planned stays with the other parent on the calendar. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Just watch**
- Any world has a new "Just watch" switch, and "Watch a city" is a new choice when you make a world. Your persona sits out: nobody texts you first, no chat is expected, and Matchlight and invitations pause. Turn it off and you pick up where you are.
- The app picks who to follow first, the companion you talk to most or a lively townsperson. Switch any time from the Focus picker or someone's profile to see their side of the same day.
- The Watching feed tells their day as it happened: work, outings, news, dreams, big turns, their child's milestones, and plans made with you that went ahead. With background work using the model on, a short daily entry is written in their voice.
- Feelings come through in words, not numbers, unless you show Hidden values.
- "Step in" starts a chat with them, or lets you become a townsperson.
- A new Inner voice slider on the Realism tab sets how much of what's on their mind the feed tells, from just what happened to everything.
- The world runs in real time while you watch. Debug time speeds it up.

**Ships and stations**
- A city can now be a ship or a station. Aboard there's no weather and no Earth seasons, nobody owns a car, and pictures get ship lighting, with corridor lights dimmed for ship's night.
- More than 120 new jobs fit a future era, from hydroponics tech to pilot to quartermaster, along with dishes, pastimes and savings goals that fit life aboard.
- Made-up places can list their neighbours with a travel time, so trips, postcards and long distance work between ships, stations and your own cities, by shuttle.
- Funerals aboard follow the ship's own customs in its chapel.

**The Long Meridian**
- A built-in generation ship of about twenty-six thousand people, 188 years out from Earth, with family cabins on spun rings, a forest deck, a night market and Port Vesper three hours away by shuttle.
- "Start from a ship" in Settings > Cities gives you a ship to make your own.
- Featured Cities are now "Featured in v0.16": The Long Meridian, Baltimore, Grandport and Osaka. Pellmouth moves to the main list.

**Memes and GIFs**
- Companions send memes even without a picture model, from a built-in starter pack, classic meme formats fetched the first time they're used, and your own pictures in the meme templates folder. With a picture model set up, they mix in made pictures too.
- Reaction GIFs from GIPHY: add your own GIPHY key in Settings > Images. Only a short word is sent to find a GIF, and only G and PG ones come back.
- A new Memes and GIFs slider on the Realism tab sets how often they send one.

**Visits with the other parent**
- When children have two homes, the app plans their stays with the other parent: after a breakup with you, a companion with their own ex, and kids from before.
- Stays show on the calendar and Today, with "Move" and "Skip this one". Your companion knows whose weekend it is.
- A stay in another city works like a trip, with the handover at the station, or at the shuttle dock aboard. A visit asked for in chat books a stay.

## What's fixed

- People in UK cities of today say "mum".
- In 1925 cities, a few jobs are worked by men only, as they were, and people work jobs that fit their age.
- In 1895 cities, only people who had the vote go to the polling station.
- Life admin costs say what was done, and the Money panel calls them "On errands and paperwork".
- The odds of a companion's own baby name the couple.
- Adopting or fostering a baby brings baby things to Life admin too.
- The Backstories box for a group says "you".

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-0.16.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-0.16.0-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale or home Wi-Fi is new, and testers have had trouble getting a phone to load. The new checks in Settings > Phone access say what's wrong.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** the launch buttons with real installs of LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp and ComfyUI, instant local replies, looking back for older memories with real models, voice notes with real voices, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **GIPHY and pictures aboard ship** haven't been tried with a real GIPHY key or real ComfyUI pictures of the ship yet.
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
