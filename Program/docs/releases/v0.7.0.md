Shuffle the deck. Every day now has a little something in it: a moment drawn from the Life deck, sometimes an odd dream the night after, and your companion knows about both. Step into someone else's life by becoming a person you've met around town, let your companions look back further when you bring up the past, and say things out of character without breaking the story. A new Realism tab puts everything that makes the world feel lifelike in one place. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Life deck: little things every day**
- Each day can draw a small moment from the Life deck: money found in an old pocket, a stranger's compliment, a recipe that finally worked. It uses the same fixed dice as the rest of their life, so a day never changes after the fact.
- Your companion knows what happened to them, and it can show up in what's on their mind or in a first message.
- Today lists the week's **Little things**. With Hidden values > odds on, each one shows a small card with its chance, and **Draw another card** sends the day another way.

**Dreams**
- Some nights a companion's day turns into a short, odd dream made from the people and places they saw. They remember it the morning after, Today lists it, and once you're past strangers it can turn into a morning text.
- Dreams never give away a secret and are never romantic, and you won't see the same dream twice in three weeks.

**See through their eyes**
- On a townsperson's card, **Become** them: a new world starts with them as your persona, in the same town with the same people. You start out with a companion from their own circle who already knows them. Your old world stays as it was.

**They can ask to remember more**
- When you bring up something from a while back, your companion looks further into their memories first, with no extra model call. If that still isn't enough, they can quietly ask to look again once before answering.
- Turn it off with **Let them look back when they need to** in Settings.

**Out of character, to the sidecar**
- Messages marked out of character (`OOC:` or `((like this))`) now go to the sidecar helper instead of your companion, and never into the story. A short line in the chat says where your aside went.
- You can change the markers in Settings > General > Out-of-character messages.
- On a computer, pop the sidecar out into its own window and put it back whenever you like. It remembers its size and place.

**Realism in one place**
- A new **Settings > Realism** tab gathers everything that makes the world feel like real life: how much drama there is, whether companions reply at their own pace, whether their days go off plan, how close you get with each companion, and Hidden values.
- Start from one of four presets, on the welcome screen or at the top of the tab: **I want real life**, **Bring on the drama**, **I want an easy life** or **I pull the strings**. Then adjust anything you like.
- Closeness can now be changed there for any companion, not only the one that's open.

## What's fixed

- Everything in v0.6.1 is included.
- A reply cut off partway through looking back no longer shows the unfinished request text.
- On a phone, the **Open** button after an aside no longer slips away before your tap lands.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-0.7.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-0.7.0-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale or home Wi-Fi is new, and testers have had trouble getting a phone to load. The new checks in Settings > Phone access say what's wrong.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** the launch buttons with real installs of LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp and ComfyUI, instant local replies, looking back for older memories with real models, voice notes with real voices, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
