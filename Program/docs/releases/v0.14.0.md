Years go by. Everyone in your world now gets older, one birthday at a time, and their lives take bigger turns: going back to school, changing careers, buying a place, starting a business, a health scare, caring for a parent, retiring. The people around them have big days you're invited to, babies arrive in the circle, the town slowly changes hands, and if you turn it on, people can pass away and be grieved. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Everyone gets older**
- Companions, the people in their lives and the townsfolk all age from today, one birthday at a time. Nobody's age jumps when you update.
- Round-number birthdays (30, 40, 50 and on) are parties and go in the scrapbook.
- Looks change slowly with age: first grey hairs, laugh lines, reading glasses. The looks you wrote are never touched.
- Turn on **Keeps their age** on a Character page for someone who shouldn't get older.

**Bigger life chapters**
- Companions can go back to school (classes, exams and a graduation), change careers, buy a place with a mortgage (together, if you live together), or retire when they're older.
- On Real life and Drama they can also start a business that grows, stalls or folds, face a health turn they learn to live with, or care for a parent who needs them.
- Each one runs over months and changes their week, their money and their home. They tell you about it the way a friend or partner would, and their Character page shows their life lately.

**Big days in the circle**
- Weddings, graduations, retirement parties and milestone birthdays in their circle are real events. They bring a gift, pick an outfit and may ask you along as their plus-one.
- The day is an outing in person. If you live far apart it becomes a visit. Saying no is fine, and they remember.
- When a friend gets married, they may ask you to stand up with them.

**Growing families**
- People in their circle have babies, with the news, a due month and then the birth and a name.
- The baby joins the circle and grows up. Your companion becomes an aunt, uncle or godparent, babysits now and then and goes to first birthdays.
- Children only ever appear in words, never in pictures. A companion's own children come with the Family release.

**Loss and grief**
- Turn on **People can pass away** on the Realism tab if you want it. It's off on every preset, and it never takes a companion, a partner or a child.
- Mostly it's the old, and more often after a long illness. People you wrote into a character are spared unless you say otherwise.
- A death brings a funeral by the city's customs, an obituary in the town paper and an anniversary. Grief lifts slowly over weeks, with fewer plans and quieter texts while it lasts. Those who passed away are kept under Remembered.

**The town changes hands**
- Townsfolk move away, take other jobs, retire and have babies, and newcomers take their place at the bakery and on the street.
- The town paper reports who arrived and who left, and a companion who knew them mentions it.
- You, the companions and the named characters from city packs stay put.

**More Realism settings**
- Settings > Realism has new **Life's big turns** and **Growing families** sliders, the **People can pass away** switch and, once it's on, a **Loss** slider. The presets set them for you, and Gentle brings good turns only.
- **Rough patches** now sits beside **Breaks and breakups**.

## What's fixed

- If you live in different cities, meeting their people and the proposal now happen during your next stay with them, instead of waiting for you to share a city.
- After a breakup, the people who met you cool on you too.
- A plain "sorry" counts as an apology, and "this weekend" asked on a Saturday means Sunday.
- Pay in modern cities is closer to real wages.
- Shared memories are listed newest first.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-0.14.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-0.14.0-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale or home Wi-Fi is new, and testers have had trouble getting a phone to load. The new checks in Settings > Phone access say what's wrong.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** the launch buttons with real installs of LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp and ComfyUI, instant local replies, looking back for older memories with real models, voice notes with real voices, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
