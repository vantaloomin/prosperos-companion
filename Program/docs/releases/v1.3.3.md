A fix-up update to 1.3, "A Sea-Change", from what testers ran into on long dates and busy days: moods that flare and fade more like a person's, replies that remember what today held, photos that match what your companion says they sent, and a few things that read wrong on Today, in posts and in Memories. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Moods that build up, and that talking clears**
- A single sharp word no longer flips your companion straight to angry. With **Rough patches** on True to life, the first sting leaves them a bit short with you, a warning sign you can talk through, and only a second one that day really hurts. Off means words never upset them, Blows over means a little irritable at most, and Dramatic keeps the old quick temper.
- Teasing never stings: a wink, a grin, a laugh, or a joke the two of you already share (one they remember) is taken as banter.
- "I don't care, you pick" is no longer taken as an insult. "I don't care" stings only on its own or about them.
- When your companion is upset with you and you talk it through, their own "we're okay" now counts: they're calm again for the rest of the day instead of staying upset until midnight. "We're okay, I just need a little time" eases things a step. More ways of saying sorry, like "my bad", are heard too.

**Long dates keep their thread**
- On a long date together in person, the start of the evening stays with your companion: where you met, what you ate, the little rules you set. Two hours in they no longer forget it or eat the same skewers again "still warm".
- Half an hour in, they know how long you've been out and where you've been the whole time, so they don't act like they've just arrived.
- Memories and earlier moments they recall now carry the time of day, so something from an hour ago doesn't read as happening right now.

**Their day, as it actually went**
- Replies and first texts know what today has held so far and the photos they sent you today. If they sent a photo from a yoga class this morning, they won't tell you it's combat training day.
- Errands and appointments like a big clean stay clear of time promised to people (Sunday with family, an evening with friends) and no longer run into work or sleep. Ones already sitting there move by themselves.

**Photos that show what they said**
- When your companion writes what a photo shows, like "quick photo: two crates by the closed drink stall", the picture is drawn from those words, through their own eyes, or as a selfie when they're in it. It stays in the chat and no longer turns up again on an unrelated post.

**Posts, status lines and Memories**
- **Posts** (or your world's word for it, like Chronicle in Camelot) is back in the left bar on a computer, right under Chats, showing the whole town. A dot shows when there's something new. On a profile, Posts opens on your companion's own posts.
- The status under your companion's name no longer shares friends' private news ("Kaito got dumped..."). It says something vague like "been a day" instead. Their own news still shows.
- Day posts no longer leave a stray "he" or "she" with nobody it could mean, and a late start or a change of plans reads as a sentence ("Traffic was a mess, so Sett ran 12 minutes late.").
- Saved memories call you "you", never "the user" or your companion's own pronoun. Memories already saved that way are fixed once, and ones you corrected yourself are left alone.
- "On their mind" on Today follows a little thing that was drawn again, instead of keeping the old thought.

**Money and home**
- Today's money card says what period its costs are for ("Each month: everyday costs about...").
- Rent is set when your companion moves in and stays put. A raise or new pay no longer changes it until they move.
- Pay decides how roomy a place they pick: someone paid well in a cheap part of town takes a bigger place, not a single room.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.3.3-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.3.3-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **A photo drawn from your companion's words has been tried on two hosted image models.** Results on local image setups may vary.
- **A day post can still add a detail the day didn't have** (like "at the office" on a night off). The stray pronoun is fixed; the made-up detail is next.
- **Something your companion already remembers from an earlier mix-up stays remembered.** If they saved a memory about a little thing that shouldn't have happened, remove it under **Memories** on their profile.
- **Finding Claude Code and Codex accounts has only been tried with stand-in command-line tools.** If an account you use isn't listed, type its folder under "Type an account folder instead".
- **Back when goes back to 1990 at the earliest,** and only real present-day cities take on a decade. Period and made-up cities keep their own era.
- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
