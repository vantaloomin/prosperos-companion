A second follow-up to 1.5, "A Most Majestic Vision", from a week of playing it on real models: your companion's chat is face to face while the two of you are in a Story scene, a whisper stays a whisper everywhere, a reply that runs out of room ends cleanly, Story costs much less on Claude's API, and texts that start from something you once told them are rarer and make sense. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Their chat knows you're together**
- While you're with your companion in a Story scene, their chat is face to face. They answer at once, as someone in the room with you, where before they wrote as if texting from across town.
- The box to write in says "Talk to Mira", and the header and chat list say "Together at Club Charles", with a book where an outing has a pin.
- Saying goodbye in their chat ends the visit, the way it ends an outing, and they go back to texting.
- On a phone, the chat header shows the place alone while you're together. It used to be cut down to "Together …".

**A whisper stays a whisper**
- What you whisper to someone else in a scene no longer turns up in your companion's texts afterward. Their chat is told only what they heard.
- A companion who was there but didn't hear it can't give it away either, even when you turn and ask them. They know something passed between you and the other person, and not what it was. This holds after the story moves somewhere else.
- A whisper to your companion is still theirs to remember.

**Replies that run out of room**
- A Story reply that reached the "Longest reply" limit used to stop wherever the limit fell and be saved as if it were finished. It now ends on its last full sentence, and a quiet line under it says "Cut short at the longest reply limit", with a link to the setting, next to **Another reply**.
- Some models think before they answer, and that thinking counts against the limit. The app already asked such a model to think less after one reply was cut, but forgot when it restarted. It now remembers, so the first long reply after a restart isn't cut again. A thinking setting you pick yourself for the model still wins.

**Story costs less on Claude's API**
- Story prompts now put what stays the same first, so it can be reused from one line to the next. On a long-running world with Claude Sonnet, a line with your companion in the scene went from about 5 cents to about 1.5, and a line with the narrator alone from about 3 cents to about 1.
- The first line of a visit still costs the full amount, and the reuse lasts five minutes between lines, which is Claude's own rule.
- No words in the prompts changed, only their order. In a blind test on twelve models the replies read the same or a little better. A local model reuses the start of a prompt by itself, so the new order suits it too.

**Texts that start from something you told them**
- Your companion's day can bring something you once told them to mind and start a text. These used to be joined by one stray word, so lost keys could "remind" them of nearly anything. It now takes a real link between their day and the memory, so these texts are rarer and make sense.
- They now know when the memory is from. Something from three weeks ago is no longer told as if it happened last night.
- The same memory isn't brought up again for a week.

**Plans and work**
- "Tuesday is a mess for me but do you want to get tea Friday at 4?" is now a plan asked for Friday. It used to be read as asked for Tuesday.
- If your world is from before 1.5 and your companion had already said a work project was finished, the first reply after updating now knows it. Before, that reply could say the project was still going, and they could keep saying so for days.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.5.2-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.5.2-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Story only follows you to places the map knows, and to your own home.** A ride somewhere, someone else's home or a place the city doesn't have isn't followed, so pick it under **Go somewhere else…**.
- **Nobody overhears a whisper yet,** whatever the Drama level.
- **A plan played out in Story that ends early keeps "Together at …" in their chat** until the plan's hour is up, even after the scene has moved on.
- **A plan for tea can still be called coffee** on Today and in the memory of it.
- **A small model can still tell an old memory as if it were recent** now and then, in a text that starts from something you told them.
- **Work projects don't follow where someone stands at work yet.** In a made-up world, a project can come from the wrong trade, like paperwork for someone who runs something else entirely.
- **A place called by a loose name may not be found,** so a plan goes somewhere nearby instead.
- **Dinner at home doesn't follow the city's food yet,** so a made-up city can get a dish from somewhere else.
- **A day post can still add a detail the day didn't have** (like "at the office" on a night off).
- **Something your companion already remembers from an earlier mix-up stays remembered.** If they saved a memory about a little thing that shouldn't have happened, remove it under **Memories** on their profile.
- **Finding Claude Code and Codex accounts has only been tried with stand-in command-line tools.** If an account you use isn't listed, type its folder under "Type an account folder instead".
- **Back when goes back to 1990 at the earliest,** and only real present-day cities take on a decade. Period and made-up cities keep their own era.
- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
