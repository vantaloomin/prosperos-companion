A fixes update to v0.8.0, "Out on the Town", from a full test of every new feature. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's fixed

**Going out together**
- Staying in no longer counts as going out. "Come over, I'll cook", a movie over a video call, or "let me tell you about my coffee disaster" stay what they are.
- A vague "we should grab coffee sometime" stays a wish instead of being booked for tomorrow.
- A plan for "Saturday, November 14" lands on November 14, not this Saturday.
- The place you name is the place you go, and free places don't cost anything.
- Undo no longer leaves two outings on the same day, and an outing won't run into their work shift.

**Trips, traditions and money**
- Trips no longer land on a day you've already planned or on a family tradition, and outings stay off tradition days too.
- Trips check what they can really afford, after rent and what they've already spent.
- The postcard from a trip arrives on its own, without waiting for you to text first.
- Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve are days off for families who keep them.
- A tradition you reword keeps their own voice, and the decorations stay the same all season.

**Maps and Who knows who**
- Plans and trips no longer show up as places they've been.
- New York 1925 gets a drawn map instead of today's streets, and period and fantasy places no longer get modern spots.
- If the street map stalls, the drawn map takes over instead of a blank space.
- Map pins open with Enter or Space, and Go there hides when you're already there.
- The close-circle filter in Who knows who shows just that circle again, not everyone.

**Chats and characters**
- Chats open at the newest message again instead of somewhere above it.
- A reply they're saving for later no longer shows "Delivered" under your message. Like a person who hasn't answered yet, it shows nothing.
- In a narrow window, the message buttons in Bubbles sit above the bubble instead of covering the text, and the heart in Retro group chats stays on its line.
- A city pack's own named characters can't become your first companion in a new world by accident.
- The Looks form says "none" for a feature left blank.
- A new day starts about twice as fast as in v0.8.0, with exactly the same results.

**Notes fix**
- The v0.8.0 notes said Osaka has the lunar holidays. Only Jeju does.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-0.8.1-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-0.8.1-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale or home Wi-Fi is new, and testers have had trouble getting a phone to load. The new checks in Settings > Phone access say what's wrong.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** the launch buttons with real installs of LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp and ComfyUI, instant local replies, looking back for older memories with real models, voice notes with real voices, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
