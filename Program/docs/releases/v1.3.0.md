"Nothing of him that doth fade, but doth suffer a sea-change into something rich and strange." Prospero's Companion 1.3 gets a quieter, simpler look on every screen, and your companion can now go away with you: long weekends and weeks away, somewhere that fits their world. Dates can also move on to a second place nearby. It folds in every fix planned for 1.2.3 as well. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**A simpler look**
- Every screen has been cleared out: fewer taglines and helper lines, labels right beside their controls, and empty sections that stay out of the way until there's something in them.
- The Paper theme is a quieter cream.
- The rail and the phone tab bar are down to Chats, Today, Matchlight and Settings, plus your world.
- **Chats:** on a computer the chat list sits beside the chat, with groups in the same list. Hide it with the panel button or the **[** key and the chat centers in the window. The app remembers your choice on each device, and a dot shows new messages while it's hidden.
- **The chat itself:** the header keeps four buttons, and everything else (chat style previews included) is under **⋯**. The message box has a **+** for sending a picture and writing help. The AI notice now sits at the top of each chat.
- **Their profile:** click their name to open it. Posts, Memories and Character live there now, and Character reads as labels and fields that fold open.
- **Closeness** shows the stage you're at, a five-step meter and the stage's description, with Adjust rows to change it. The stage names are the same as before.
- **Today** opens with a Now card that shows their day as a strip, with the day's tools in the header.
- **Worlds** is a list with the picked world beside it: a check on the one you're in, **⋯** for rename, copy and pause, Just watch as a switch, and the people here in a grid.
- **Settings** is eight groups plus Advanced, all laid out as rows. Realism, Pictures and World follow the same pattern: picture models fold open to show their details, Adult and Hidden values have their own pages, and Cities and Library are pages under World.
- **Model profiles** follow the same pattern. Provider is one list, and Generation settings folds open with its context and reply sizes shown on the right. Context size and time limit are picked from presets, and the longest reply is a slider. Claude Code and Codex accounts are rows to pick from, and the switch that looks for them sits on the Models page.

**Away together**
- Ask your companion to go away with you, right in the chat: a long weekend or a week somewhere. They say yes, or tell you why not, like money, work or that it's too soon.
- They suggest trips of their own too, once you're close enough, somewhere they can afford. How often follows the Getting together slider.
- Places fit their world: real destinations of the right years in a real city (nobody flies somewhere that wasn't a destination yet in a 1995 world), and getaways of their own for made-up worlds and ships.
- Each of you pays your own way, and a week away uses their vacation days.
- A trip shows on Today in Coming up and then in the Now card while you're away, with Somewhere else, Call it off and Head home (each can be undone).

**Second location**
- A date or meetup in town can move on to a second place nearby when the first one ends, like drinks after dinner or a walk on a warm evening. The app picks the place: around the corner, open at that hour and in season, something they like and can afford, and right for their world and era.
- It only happens when they have the time afterward. You can also just ask in the chat ("a drink after this?") and they'll go on somewhere with you.
- The Where strip and Today show the move. Tap **Somewhere else?** to pick another place, **Stay longer** to stay put, or **Back to** the first place for two minutes after you've moved on.
- A new **Moving on** slider in Settings > Realism sets how often it happens, from never to often.

**Fixes**
- **Dates they remember:** later the same evening, your companion remembers the outing you had together that day, and the first time the two of you went out is remembered as the first.
- **Memories:** saved memories use your companion's name and pronouns, never "the assistant", and plans and relationship memories say who did what ("You…").
- **New in Feed:** Today's "new in Feed" goes straight to the new post, even when it sits below posts you've already seen, and the feed's "new" count is a link to it. Very tall posts count as seen.
- **Message box:** after you send a long message, the box goes back to its usual size, in every chat style and in Story.
- **Claude Code and Codex accounts:** a Claude Code or Codex profile in Settings > Models now lists the accounts it finds on this computer, such as a second `claude2` command, so you can pick one. It goes by command and folder names, asks the CLI whether each one is signed in, and never opens your sign-in files. You can turn this off.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.3.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.3.0-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Finding Claude Code and Codex accounts has only been tried with stand-in command-line tools.** If an account you use isn't listed, type its folder under "Type an account folder instead".
- **Your picture and pictures that follow a profile picture haven't been tried on every image setup yet.** They're tested with stand-ins; results on local edit models and hosted services may vary.
- **Back when goes back to 1990 at the earliest,** and only real present-day cities take on a decade. Period and made-up cities keep their own era.
- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
