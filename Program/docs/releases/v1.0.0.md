Our revels now are ended, and this is the curtain call: Prospero's Companion 1.0. Period cities now speak in their own words, so in Camelot you write a letter, in 1895 a note is sent round, and aboard ship you open a comm. Companions keep sounding like themselves, a reference library lets them know a book or a whole wiki, lore turns up when the moment fits, and the Watching feed now follows love lives too. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Words that fit the era**
- In period cities, the world uses period words: the chat, Today, Watching, the circle, the matchmaker's page, the feed, notifications and the app's own lines. Camelot has letters and a scrying glass, 1895 has notes, the telephone and the Personals, 1925 New York has the telephone, and ships and stations have comms and credits.
- Each era has its own words, and the built-in period cities and both ships add their own where the era's aren't quite right. City files can rename any word with a `words` block.
- Settings, setup and help keep today's words, so they stay easy to follow.
- "Period words in period cities" in Settings > Appearance turns it off. It's on by default.

**Their voice stays theirs**
- Companions now hear a few short lines of what they've said before, picked from replies you kept, so they go on sounding like the same person.
- "Pin their voice" in a reply's menu keeps up to three replies you think sound most like them. Pins win over the app's picks, and you can unpin from the reply or the Character page.
- In group chats, each person hears their own lines.

**A reference library**
- Give a companion, or the whole world, a long text to know: a novel, a script, transcripts or a wiki export. Text, Markdown, web pages, EPUB and TiddlyWiki files all work.
- Each reply draws on at most three short passages that match what you're talking about, without repeating what it just used. The companion knows it as their own knowledge, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- The Library sits beside Lore on the Character page, and world books are in Settings. Each book can be turned off, and its passages can be searched.
- Grandport has "Add the Neokosmos wiki", which downloads Oak's wiki into the library, and "Get the latest from Oak's wiki" to keep up with Oak's updates. Credit to Oak, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

**Lore that fits the moment**
- A lore entry can come up when the moment fits: in the rain or snow, a season, a time of day, weekends, a holiday, a place, or at work. Aboard ship, ship's night counts as night.
- Imported lorebooks get a "when" where their keywords already name one, like an entry keyed "winter". It shows as a chip you can remove.
- The Lore screen shows what fits right now and lets you add or remove when an entry comes up.
- Thirteen built-in cities have lore of their own for their weather, seasons, holidays and places, and city files can carry a `lore` block.

**Love life in the Watching feed**
- Getting together, moving in, engagements, weddings, rough patches, breaks, breakups, falling out and making up are now told in the Watching feed on the day.
- Heartache gets thoughts written for it.
- The line under a name follows how things stand: "Your companion", "Your ex", "On a break" or "Not speaking", with their job beside it.

**Come say hello**
- Settings now has a link to the Discord, [Dreamers AI Hub: Prospero's Suite](https://discord.gg/sdAtBBjvFA), under the tabs on a computer and at the foot of Settings on a phone.

## What's fixed

- In period cities, nobody gets stuck on a phone call or cracks a phone screen any more. Running late and plans falling through fit the era, and a birthday call out of town is a letter.
- An inner-voice line about letting go of worry reads right.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.0.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.0.0-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **The library matches by meaning only with an embedding model.** Without one it matches on the words you use, which works but is stricter. EPUB files from the wild haven't been tried yet.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale or home Wi-Fi is new, and testers have had trouble getting a phone to load. The new checks in Settings > Phone access say what's wrong.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** the launch buttons with real installs of LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp and ComfyUI, instant local replies, looking back for older memories with real models, voice notes with real voices, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **GIPHY and pictures aboard ship** haven't been tried with a real GIPHY key or real ComfyUI pictures of the ship yet.
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
