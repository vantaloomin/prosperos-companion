A small fix-up update to 1.3, "A Sea-Change", from what testers ran into this week: texts your companion sends first, their own birthday, money in cities you wrote, dreams that fit the world, and who turns up in the little things on Today. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Another reply on texts they send first**
- When your companion texts you by themselves, like a check-in after a quiet spell or a follow-up on a plan, that text now has **Another reply** while it's their newest message. The new version comes from the same reason and from the chat as it was before it.

**Their own birthday**
- Your companion always knows their own birthday, the one on their Character page, instead of making one up when you ask.

**Money in cities you wrote**
- Today's budget now works in cities written in the app and in cities brought in from an imported world map. Before, Today asked you to pick a home city there. Editing a city's rents is picked up straight away.

**Dreams that fit the world**
- Dreams take their odd touches and things from the companion's world: modern, historical, fantasy or future. No more airports in Camelot, and train and museum dreams stay out of fantasy worlds.

**The little things on Today**
- The small moments on Today only bring in people your companion actually knows. Someone who only knows of them, like a fan they've never met, no longer sends an old photo "of the two of them from years ago".
- An old photo or an old inside joke comes from someone they've known for years: an old friend, family, a partner, or someone whose note says so.
- A moment already drawn with someone who doesn't fit any more is drawn again.

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.3.2-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.3.2-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Something your companion already remembers from an earlier mix-up stays remembered.** If they saved a memory about a little thing that shouldn't have happened (like that old photo), remove it under **Memories** on their profile.
- **Finding Claude Code and Codex accounts has only been tried with stand-in command-line tools.** If an account you use isn't listed, type its folder under "Type an account folder instead".
- **Your picture and pictures that follow a profile picture haven't been tried on every image setup yet.** They're tested with stand-ins; results on local edit models and hosted services may vary.
- **Back when goes back to 1990 at the earliest,** and only real present-day cities take on a decade. Period and made-up cities keep their own era.
- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
