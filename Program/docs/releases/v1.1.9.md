A small update to 1.1, "Such People In't", with local politics, more control over pictures, and requests from testers. If you have an earlier version, install over it (or run `update.bat` / `update.command` in a source copy). Your worlds, companions and everything they remember are kept, and the first launch makes a verified backup before updating.

## What's new

**Talk of the town: local politics**
- Every city now has things people argue about. Today has a new **Talk of the town**: the big questions of its era (like Prohibition in New York 1925, which never settles against history) and local ones, from a mayor and the orphanage money to a license or a new pier. Each one moves from whispers to open to heated to settled.
- Factions back sides, gain or lose pull as they win and lose, and drift between allies and rivals. Grandport and New York 1925 come with their own factions and questions.
- Everyone has a view, worked out from their work, the words on their card, their temperament and the factions they belong to. Close people who feel strongly can nudge your companion. You can change any view, rename or settle an issue, add or put away issues and factions, say who belongs, which side a faction backs and how two factions get on.
- Your companion knows what the town is talking about in chat, and the town paper has a Politics block.
- Settings > Realism has a new **Local politics** slider: off, quiet, true to life (the default) and lively.
- **Real local headlines (optional):** for a real, present-day city, turn on **Local politics in (their name)'s city** on the built-in local pulse (or "For the companion's city" on any news tool) and that city's political headlines are read twice a day and show up in Talk of the town. It's off by default.

**Pictures**
- **Every OpenRouter image model:** Settings > Images now lists and uses all of OpenRouter's image models, including FLUX, Seedream, Recraft and Krea, not just the Google and OpenAI ones.
- **Your own picture as their reference:** Profile pictures can now start from **Use your own picture** (PNG, JPEG or WebP) instead of the description. Pictures 2 and 3 are made from it, and pictures of your companion now follow their profile picture on every backend that can take a reference picture, not only Codex. Settings > Images has **Keep their face from their profile picture** to turn this off for a world.
- **New picture or Remove picture** on a picture your companion sent in chat. With a mouse the buttons show on the picture; on a phone, press and hold it. Removing keeps the message and has Undo.
- **Krea 2 on smaller graphics cards:** the built-in Krea 2 setup now picks a model file that fits your card (NVFP4 on RTX 50-series cards, or a GGUF file), says which file fits and where it goes, and offers a one-press switch. GGUF model files work now.
- **One look per picture:** what you wrote in Appearance and In photos now wins over a drawn look, so a "tall, muscular man in his early thirties" no longer comes out stocky and twenty-five. A scene written into In photos no longer turns up in every picture, an outfit written there is what they wear, a picture of them alone shows only them, and they are only drawn at home when they're doing something done at home.
- **Use their name in pictures:** a new per-character switch adds their name to picture prompts. It's off by default.
- **Clearer picture errors:** a hosted image service's own error message now comes through, and ComfyUI says which model file is missing and which folder it belongs in. When a hosted image API or Codex fails, a local ComfyUI you have set up tries instead.

**Each world its own way**
- Life & cities > This world can give a world its own drama level, instead of the one in Realism. A companion alone in their world gets storylines a little more often.
- A world can pick its own image backends (Settings > Images > Pictures in (world name)) and its own text models (Settings > Models > Models in (world name)). Until you do, it uses the usual ones.

**Homes and the map**
- Their home is placed in the neighborhood their description, location and card say ("above the fighting pits" is The Pits), and you can move it on the map with **Edit home**.
- What's inside a place is editable with **Edit inside**, and their days, pictures and Story mode use the rooms and spots you give.
- The map shows your home and the homes of other companions and circle people in the city.
- Home quirks can be edited and removed, are cleared when you write a new description, and fit ship life aboard.

**Their people**
- Someone you add by hand starts where their words say: never met is Just met, known for years is Comfortable. The Edit form can set where they start.
- Family no longer becomes friends with the rest of the circle by chance.
- Each person's Edit view lists who they know in the circle, and you can change how, add a link or take one away.
- On your posts, a circle person reacts by how close your companion is to them too, and their own people like and comment now and then.

**Other fixes**
- A reply held while your companion was busy now shows the time it arrived, not the time you wrote.
- Who they are, job, traits, looks and time zone boxes use the app's own suggestion list instead of the browser's popup, which looked like the browser offering old typing. Time zones are easier to search ("new york" finds New York).

## Install on Windows

**Installer (easiest):** download `ProsperoCompanion-1.1.9-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights, Python or Node. It adds Prospero Companion to the Start menu. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable:** `ProsperoCompanion-1.1.9-win-x64.zip` is the same app without an installer. Unzip it anywhere and double-click `Prospero Companion.cmd` in the `ProsperoCompanion` folder.

**From source:** download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Windows` folder, double-click `install.bat` once and then `launch.bat`. It can update itself later with `update.bat`, whether you cloned it or downloaded the ZIP.

Then open **Settings > Models**, add a model, and create your companion. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.

## Install on a Mac

There's no Mac app yet. Download the source code below (or `git clone` the repository), open the `Mac` folder and double-click `install.command`, then `launch.command`. It needs Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22.13+ and installs them with Homebrew if you have it. The scripts aren't signed, so a downloaded ZIP needs **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security the first time. [Installing on a Mac](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-companion/blob/main/Program/docs/macos.md) walks through it.

By default your companion's data lives outside the program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProsperoCompanion` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/ProsperoCompanion` on a Mac), so updating or reinstalling keeps it. Settings > Data shows where yours is.

## Known issues

- **Set it up for me hasn't been run on real hardware yet.** It has been tested end to end with a stand-in download and a stand-in graphics card. The bigger models are up to about 17 GB, so the first download can take a while.
- **Built-in recall needs a model file you download yourself.** The Companion suggests EmbeddingGemma 2 and Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B but never downloads a model for you. The llama.cpp builds it fetches for Mac haven't been run on a real Mac yet. On Linux, point `COMPANION_LLAMA_SERVER` at your own llama-server.
- **The library matches by meaning only with an embedding model.** Without one it matches on the words you use, which works but is stricter. EPUB files from the wild haven't been tried yet.
- **Not code-signed.** Expect SmartScreen warnings on Windows and a Gatekeeper block on the first Mac run.
- **Mac is untested on real hardware.** The scripts and tests pass on GitHub's Apple Silicon and Intel Macs only. LoRA training isn't available on a Mac.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale or home Wi-Fi is new, and testers have had trouble getting a phone to load. The new checks in Settings > Phone access say what's wrong.
- **Not yet tried on real setups:** the launch buttons with real installs of LM Studio, Ollama, KoboldCpp and ComfyUI, instant local replies, looking back for older memories with real models, voice notes with real voices, Matchlight's Show photo, and the Local Pulse and Culture Pulse lookups (tested against recorded responses only).
- **GIPHY and pictures aboard ship** haven't been tried with a real GIPHY key or real ComfyUI pictures of the ship yet.
- **Krea 2 on a 12 GB card** (the NVFP4 and GGUF files) hasn't been tried on a real 12 GB card yet.
- **Image generation** depends on your own backend. Krea 2 with ComfyUI has run on one Windows PC with an RTX 5090. Text inside pictures can come out garbled.
- **Real-city data** comes from general knowledge and is labeled as estimates.
